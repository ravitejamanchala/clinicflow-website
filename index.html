GOOD TO KNOW

Clear answers.
No fine-print surprises.

Who is ClinicFlow designed for?+

UK independent medical consultants and small clinics looking to connect day-to-day administration, patient records, appointments, correspondence and follow-ups. The right scope is agreed with each practice.

Can I use this as an NHS GP system?+

Not as a replacement for an NHS GP system. Live NHS connectivity is not enabled. NHS API access requires the relevant onboarding, agreements and assurance. A mock or sandbox integration is not a production connection.

Does it connect directly to laboratories?+

Not currently. Staff can record results and link uploaded test-result files to the relevant record. Direct laboratory ordering and results ingestion are not live integrations.

What about email and SMS reminders?+

Email workflows require a configured and tested delivery provider. Live SMS delivery is not currently connected. We will confirm available channels and their setup before agreeing your pilot.

Does ClinicFlow write clinical content with AI?+

The workflow presented here is template-based and clinician-led, not an AI scribe. Staff write and review the content. Optional experimental on-device writing tools in the application are separate from this workflow and require their own evaluation.

How do we get started—and what does it cost?+

Start with a conversation about your team, workflows and current systems. We will agree the pilot scope, onboarding and pricing before you commit. This website does not take payments or collect patient information.
